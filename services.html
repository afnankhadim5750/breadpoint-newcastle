The menu

Baked in small batches through the day, Hong Kong bakery style. Ask in store for today's full selection — when it's gone, it's gone.

Street favourite

Bubble Waffles

The Hong Kong street-snack icon: a crisp, golden lattice of little bubbles with a soft, chewy middle, griddled to order and served warm in paper.

  • Made fresh on the iron while you wait
  • Enjoy plain, or with classic toppings like red bean and condensed milk
  • A proper handheld treat for the walk home
Signature bake

Pineapple Buns

Don't let the name fool you — there's no pineapple inside. These beloved buns are named for their crackled, golden crust, hiding a soft, buttery crumb beneath.

  • Baked through the day in small batches
  • Best eaten warm, straight from the tray
  • The taste Hong Kong bakeries are famous for
Baked daily

Egg Tarts

Flaky, buttery pastry cradling a silky baked custard with a whisper of caramel on top. Delicate, wobbly, and gone far too quickly.

  • Flaky pastry, silky just-set custard
  • A two-bite classic with tea or coffee
  • Box a few to take home — you won't regret it
Made to order

Celebration Cakes

Light-as-air sponge layered with fresh cream and fruit — the Chinese-bakery style cake that's made birthdays special for generations.

  • Made to order for birthdays and occasions
  • Light sponge, fresh cream, seasonal fruit
  • Call 0191 221 0229 ahead to arrange yours

Our story

A little taste of Hong Kong on Low Friar Street

Breadpoint opened its doors in 2016, bringing the flavours of a Hong Kong bakery to the heart of Newcastle upon Tyne. Part artisan bakery, part neighbourhood café, it is the kind of place where the smell of fresh buns pulls you in off the street.

Everything is baked in small batches through the day — pineapple buns with their signature crackled tops, silky egg tarts, bubble waffles made to order — alongside proper tea and coffee in a warm, easygoing café setting.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where exactly is Breadpoint?
You'll find us at 13 Low Friar Street, Newcastle upon Tyne NE1 5UE — in the city centre, a short walk from Central Station.
What are your opening hours?
We're open Monday to Friday, 10:30am to 5pm, and Saturday to Sunday, 11am to 5pm — seven days a week.
Do I need to book a table?
No need — just pop in. Walk-ins are always welcome, whether you're grabbing buns to go or settling in with a tea.
Can I order a celebration cake?
Yes — our celebration cakes are made to order. Give us a call on 0191 221 0229 ahead of time and we'll arrange it with you.
Do you cater for larger orders or events?
For bigger orders, parties or office treats, call us on 0191 221 0229 and we'll talk through what's possible.
